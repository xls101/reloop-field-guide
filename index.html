      What these numbers actually mean

      EPA WARM v16 provides the selected container-glass and PET-resin carbon references (December 2023). Confirm the exact material and counted mass yourself. Generic items default to no verified baseline; the optional illustrative mode keeps the old author assumptions. Mass, quantity, prices/costs and displacement are still user assumptions, not automatically measured inputs. No path is a supplier-specific LCA or verified waste-diversion result.

      Source scenario = quantity × material mass (kg) × published MTCO₂e/US short ton × 1,000 / 907.1847 × assumed displacement / 100. Current-mix and virgin values are alternative references, not an uncertainty interval. Illustrative mode instead uses its assumed kg CO₂e/kg factor. Spending difference = quantity × (assumed new price × displacement / 100 − assumed per-item route cost). For recycling, only entered costs are counted. For unsafe and unidentified items, no metric is modelled.

      WARM glass is container/packaging glass, not windows, ceramics or drinking glasses. PET is resin only: bottle extrusion, caps and labels are excluded. US inventory assumptions and some production transportation are included; repair/cleaning/reuse travel, use, disposal and market response are not assessed. Source paths have no water factor: no green, blue, grey, scarcity-weighted or local water saving is shown. Optional illustrative litres remain uncalibrated assumptions. Never add this gross what-if to recycling credits or call it a measured/net saving.

        Privacy, offline mode & project status

        No accounts, analytics, geolocation, image recognition or API keys. The only app requests are local static assets. A selected photo is a temporary local preview, decoded by your browser; it is not sent, analysed, saved in history or embedded in reports.

        Plans persist in localStorage when the browser permits it. If storage is blocked or full, you get an explicit tab-only warning. An unreadable shelf is left untouched until you confirm a reset. Offline reload works after the service worker has cached the app on localhost or HTTPS; use the local server, not a file:// double-click.
